### Task 2: Stateful interactive stop-motion bottle

**Files:** Create `src/motion/bottle-state.js`, `src/motion/bottle.js`, `tests/bottle-state.test.mjs`; wire `src/main.js`.

**Interfaces:**
- `transitionBottle(state, event)` returns the next cap state; states `closed | popping | open | resetting`, events `activate | animationEnd`.
